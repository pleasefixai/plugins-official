# Google Search Console

Load adspirer-search-mcp. Discover google_search_console actions and schemas first.
Confirm the authorized site property (domain or URL-prefix), dates, country, device, and
search type. Select siteUrl explicitly when more than one property is available.
Use available query/page clicks, impressions, CTR, and position to propose search opportunities.
These are the connected site's organic observations, not competitor rankings, keyword market
volume, paid CPC, backlinks, or a general site-health crawl.
State aggregation/privacy limitations shown by the response.
Recommend paid-search experiments without automatically creating campaigns.
URL indexing submissions or other mutations require approval for the exact URLs and action.
Do not promise an indexing capability for every URL or site; obey live tool restrictions.
